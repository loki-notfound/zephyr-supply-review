# Post-Fork Update — Zephyr Protocol v2.0.2 (Hotfix)
**Block Height:** 433000 (HF7)


### Summary
We have successfully executed a **hotfix hard fork (v2.0.2)** at block 433000 to address an issue that allowed transactions to **spend outputs of a different asset type** without proper enforcement. In short, this bug made it possible for one asset (e.g., ZSD, ZRS, ZYS) to be **spent** as if it were another asset (e.g., ZEPH), with wallet code modifications. We have now **closed** this loophole by enforcing that ring members must strictly match on `asset_type`.

A huge thank you goes to the anonymous developer and contributor who first discovered and alerted us to this issue. They went above and beyond in investigating and helping us resolve it.

.

=== 2025-01-23T04:43:48.968Z | fennzar | https://discord.com/channels/1112605073948819559/1112605856488493176/1331846830748864512

### Impact

- **30 transactions** have been identified with cross-asset ring members, starting soon after our v1.0.0 launch (Sep 2023), which introduced ZSD and ZRS.
- In these cases, it appears likely that some ZRS and ZSD were “morphed” at a 1:1 rate into ZEPH.
- As a result, there is more ZEPH in circulation than normally accounted for, and correspondingly less ZSD and ZRS.
- The anonymous developer was able to estimate that in 10 out of these 30 transactions (due to specific patterns), roughly **95,000 ZSD** were morphed into ZEPH. For the other 20 transactions, we do not have a way to determine the total amount morphed.
- All these transactions were recorded as “ZEPH” transfers, most of which morphed ZSD → ZEPH.
- Any ZSD morphed into ZEPH is no longer exists. This bypasses the normal `redeem_stable` process which reduces the total circulation figure.
- Currently, the total reported ZSD supply is ~580,000 ZSD. The Yield Reserve holds ~370,000 ZSD, leaving ~210,000 ZSD in circulation outside the Reserve.
- Based on our understanding, the total morphed ZSD → ZEPH is likely between the 95,000 proven and up to ~210,000 not accounted for in the Yield Reserve, minus any ZSD held by the team and on exchanges.
- There are fewer transactions involving ZRS → ZEPH, and the rate difference between ZRS and ZEPH has been smaller than for ZSD and ZEPH, which makes sense as to why the focus as been morphing ZSD -> ZEPH.
Overall, we estimate ~200,000 ZEPH in “inflation” (and an equal amount of ZSD + ZRS total number in “deflation”) from this exploit—this is our **best educated guess** and not a confirmed figure. Further investigation may refine these numbers.

### Is the Issue Fixed?
Yes. As of **v2.0.2 (HF7)**, the daemon **enforces** consistent ring-member asset types. Any future transaction that attempts to mix asset types in its ring will be rejected by consensus and the code will be published shortly.

**Important note:** This issue is **distinct** from the “range proofs” issue mentioned in previous announcements, which involves three suspect transactions that fail the updated range proofs code.

- Our ongoing **in-depth chain audit** continues, aiming to quantify both this exploit’s impact and any implications from the range proofs issue.

## Reserve Reward and Yield Reward logic and effects on Reserve Ratio:
5% of the block reward is allocated for the ZSD Yield Mechanism. The ZEPH from the block reward is used to mint new ZSD, which is then placed into the Yield Reserve, while the ZEPH itself is added to the Reserve, backing these newly minted stablecoins. The amount of ZSD minted fluctuates based on ZEPH’s price at the time. The mechanism operates similarly to our proven ZRS model.

The Reserve Ratio which is defined as the total value of ZEPH backing all ZSDs. The value of ZEPH in the Reserve are our Assets, and the total circulation of ZSD is our Liabilities.”

`Reserve Ratio = Assets / Liabilities`
`Reserve Ratio = ZEPH Value in Reserve / Total ZSD Circulation`

The new block rewards will send 30% (Reserve Reward) + 5% (Yield Reward) of the ZEPH block reward to the DJED based Reserve.
The 5% Yield reward portion is used to mint the equivalent value in ZSD for the new Yield reserve.

This means there is a 7:1 Ratio of new assets to new liabilities generated from our block reward. This means that for every unit of ZSD minted as a yield reward, 7 units of ZEPH are added to the reserve. This balance ensures the reserve ratio will naturally normalize at 700% - all else equal.

As a extra precaution, ZSD Minting (for the yield reserve) will be halted below 200% Reserve Ratio, to help speed up natural protocol recovery.

## Spot/MA pricing - Newcomers please read!
I would imagine that a lot of us here in the discord are well aware of the spot/ma interaction.
**Please do your part** in ensuring that new users aren't surprised by this interaction if people talk about wanting to swap!

**For the uninitiated:**
It's important to remember that when swapping between assets (like ZEPH -> ZSD) you use the ***worst rate between the spot and ma price ***
This is an anti price manipulation measure to ensure it is not possible to game the system for profit...

But as a genuine user, you do need to be careful of swapping during times when there is a high divergence between the spot and ma price.
You can check this on the website or in your wallet!

E.G. (using prices from a little while ago)

Swapping 100 ZEPH for ZSD may be less than you expect:
ma = $4.36
spot = $4.57

This would net 433 ZSD using a moving average of $4.36 
As Apposed to 455 ZSD at a spot price of $4.57 for example

After 0.1% conversion fees.
Be sure to keep this in mind! 🫡

We have had cases of $4 spot vs $3 ma which could result in a bit of nasty surprise!
It's really a problem at the moment due to big swings in the market, when they calm down the divergence is usually not too bad

I would love in the future to adjust the moving average calc so that it is more aligned/weighted to the current price to minimize this effect, but its a tricky one because we need to ensure that the market can't be manipulated for purposes of gaming the protocol

**We are currently experiencing a network syncing issue**

TL;DR - We need to work on a fix, test and release ASAP - we are targeting to complete this over the next 24 hours.


**Nature of the issue:**
Many nodes are struggling to sync to the top height.
Although we are still investigating, it seems the issue can be traced to small discrepancies produced in the native asset prices in the pricing_record.

For instance - Height `274226` a common stuck point


```
2024-06-17 05:29:31.825 D bl.pricing_record.stable: 395144550000 bl.pricing_record.stable_ma: 385425440000 bl.pricing_record.reserve: 1248532590000 bl.pricing_record.reserve_ma: 1254829100000
2024-06-17 05:29:31.825 D stable_price: 395144550000 stable_price_ma: 385425440000 reserve_price: 1248532590000 reserve_price_ma: 1254829110000
2024-06-17 05:29:31.825 E Block with id: <23982e0750ba3709b3ebb0a666af47efd79527ed2c8f3b7f5026aa718abc8e36>
2024-06-17 05:29:31.825 E has invalid pricing record values!
```

In this case - the `bl.pricing_record.reserve_ma` value (from the block what we are trying to sync, generated from the pool) is different from what the nodes calculate this to be in `reserve_price_ma` and thus the block is rejected, resulting in us being stuck here.


```
bl.pricing_record.reserve_ma: 1254829100000
reserve_price_ma:             1254829110000 //difference of 10000
```

This check is done to ensure that the block added is acceptable, and the values aren't able to be manipulated.
In my testing thus far, **I was able to add a tolerance to allow these small differences, which does work!** But it isn't likely to be the root cause itself.

# 🚀 Zephyr Protocol - Artemis v1.2.0 Release 🚀
We are excited to announce the release of Zephyr Protocol - Artemis v1.2.0, introducing major enhancements to improve functionality, user adoption, and security. This update includes critical protocol changes, fee adjustments, and the latest Monero developments.

The Artemis v1.2.0 update comprises two hardforks; v4 and v5.


## Download:
https://github.com/ZephyrProtocol/zephyr/releases/tag/v1.2.0

**Hardfork v4**
*Activated at block 294000 - July 13th, 2024 15:00 UTC (approx)*

Activates the new pricing record format to save previous reserve ratio values in the pricing record.

**Hardfork v5**
*Activated at block 295000 - July 15th, 2024 00:00 UTC (approx)*

The reserve ratio moving average is now derived from an average of previous reserve ratio values over a 24-hour period, made possible from the pricing record changes in Hardfork v4. This method smooths out fluctuations and prevents manipulation of the reserve ratio by minting/redeeming ZSD.

**Fee Adjustments:**


- Mint ZSD: 2% → 0.1%
- Redeem ZSD: 2% → 0.1%
- Mint ZRS: 0% → 1%
- Redeem ZRS: 2% → 1%


These changes lower barriers to entry, increase ZSD circulation, and make it easier to maintain the arbitrage peg on exchanges.

Future Development:
Our next mission is to add a yield mechanism for ZSD holders. Our early concept is outlined here:
https://zephyrprotocol.com/blog/2024-05-30-Zephyr-Protocol-ZSD-Adoption-Chicken-and-Egg
